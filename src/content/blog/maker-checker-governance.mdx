# Maker-Checker Approvals for Wire Governance

Single-credential treasury accounts create catastrophic blast radius when an operator laptop is compromised. Ravfina enforces strict segregation of duties: the team member who initiates a wire or high-limit transfer (`Maker`) can never satisfy the second signature (`Checker`).

## Policy Rules Evaluated in Under 12ms

- Wires exceeding the organization threshold enter `PendingApproval` before touching settlement rails.
- Hardware FIDO2 passkeys sign both approval challenges and API key rotations.
- Every rejection or approval writes an immutable audit event streamed into the live workspace log.
