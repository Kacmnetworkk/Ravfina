# Automated Working Capital Underwriting

Traditional commercial credit lines take six weeks of PDF bank statements and manual debt-service coverage spreadsheets. Ravfina replaces static underwriting packets with continuous telemetry pulled directly from Plaid operating accounts and ERP general ledgers.

## Real-Time Risk Scoring Inputs

- **Cash Coverage Multiple**: 90-day trailing operating liquidity divided by fixed monthly burn.
- **DSO Collection Velocity**: Weighted invoice aging from NetSuite, SAP, or QuickBooks Online.
- **Guardrail Floor Trigger**: Automated drawdowns fire only when projected balance crosses the controller-configured cash floor.
